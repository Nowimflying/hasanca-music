Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Hasanca ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you interact with our website, our content management application ("Hasanca Music Bot"), and our TikTok presence (@xhasanca).

1. Information We Collect

Our internal content management tool, which integrates with the official TikTok API, may access the following data solely related to the account owner's own TikTok profile (@xhasanca):

  • Public profile information (username, display name, avatar, bio, follower/following counts)
  • Video metadata for content owned by @xhasanca (titles, descriptions, view counts, engagement metrics)
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok for authenticated API calls

We do not collect data from third-party TikTok users, viewers, or audience members beyond what is publicly aggregated by TikTok's analytics endpoints for the @xhasanca account.

2. How We Use the Information

Information accessed via the TikTok API is used exclusively for:

  • Managing and scheduling content uploads to @xhasanca
  • Reviewing performance analytics of @xhasanca's own posts
  • Improving the content strategy of the Hasanca music project

The application is private and intended for use by the @xhasanca account owner only. It is not offered as a public service.

3. Data Storage and Security

Access tokens and refresh tokens are stored locally on the account owner's personal device, in encrypted environment files. No data is transmitted to third-party servers other than TikTok's official API endpoints and the social media scheduling tool Buffer (used for cross-platform posting).

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade any information. Data accessed via the TikTok API is not shared with any third party except:

  • TikTok — for the purpose of making authenticated API requests
  • Buffer — for cross-platform post scheduling (only content the account owner explicitly schedules)

5. Third-Party Services

This application uses the TikTok API. Your interaction with TikTok is governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention

OAuth tokens are retained only as long as needed for the application to function. Tokens may be revoked at any time by the account owner via TikTok account settings. Local analytics caches are deleted when no longer needed.

7. User Rights

As this is a personal tool used by the @xhasanca account owner, no third-party user data is processed. The account owner has full control over all data and may delete, export, or revoke access at any time.

8. Children's Privacy

This service is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us via the TikTok account @xhasanca.
